Why a Course of CACI Beats a One-Off Facial

Authorised CACI practitioner since 2002 — here's what I've learned after treating thousands of clients

P
Phillippa — Authorised CACI Practitioner & Clinic Owner Freckles Beauty Clinic, 308 Linthorpe Road, Middlesbrough  ·  7 May 2026
← Back to Skin School

I get asked a version of the same question nearly every week: "Can I just book one CACI session to see if it works?" And my honest answer is always the same — yes, you absolutely can. You'll feel the difference and you'll likely see a lift. But if you're serious about results that genuinely last? A single session will only ever show you a fraction of what CACI is capable of.

I've been performing CACI treatments since 2002. In that time I've seen the difference between clients who come for a one-off and clients who commit to a course — and it's not a subtle difference. It's transformational. Let me explain why.

First — What Is CACI Actually Doing to Your Face?

CACI works by delivering precise, gentle microcurrent impulses into the facial muscles — essentially re-educating them. Think of it like a personal training session for your face. As we get older, the electrical activity in our facial muscles naturally diminishes. That's what causes the drooping, the loss of definition along the jawline, the heaviness around the eyes. CACI directly addresses that at the source.

Combined with LED light therapy, it also stimulates collagen production and accelerates the skin's own repair process — so you're getting both a structural lift and genuine skin quality improvement at the same time.

Now here's the key thing: your muscles have memory. And that memory takes time to re-establish.

What Happens in a Single Session

I want to be clear — a single CACI session is genuinely impressive. Most of my clients notice a visible lift after their very first treatment. The jawline looks more defined. The brow area feels lifted. The skin has a glow to it. It's one of the reasons people get CACI before a special occasion — a wedding, a big birthday, a reunion.

One of our lovely clients came to us before her birthday and this is what she had to say afterwards:

"I enjoyed a fantastic relaxing facial completed by Phillipa. It's always a pleasure to visit the salon — Phillipa and Katie are extremely knowledgeable, professional and make you feel so comfortable. I look forward to my next visit."

Five-star Google Review — Verified Client

That kind of result from a single session — that glow, that feeling of looking and feeling your best — is absolutely real. But here's the thing. That lift? Without a course behind it, it will fade within a few days as the muscles gradually return to their previous position. It's the same reason one session at the gym won't change your body. The results need to be built and reinforced over time.

Why a Course Changes Everything

When I recommend a course of CACI — typically ten sessions, ideally twice a week to begin with — what we're doing is progressively re-training the facial muscles to hold their new, lifted position. Each session builds on the last. The muscles get stronger. The lift becomes structural rather than temporary. And crucially, the results start to last longer and longer between sessions.

By the end of a course, my clients are seeing results that are genuinely comparable to what used to require far more invasive procedures. No needles. No recovery time. No looking unnatural. Just a more rested, lifted, defined version of yourself.

"By session five or six, clients often come in and tell me their friends have asked if they've had something done. By the end of the course, they just look like a better, fresher version of themselves — and nobody can quite put their finger on why."

— Phillippa, Authorised CACI Practitioner since 2002

The science backs this up entirely. CACI's system is backed by over 20 years of clinical research. The microcurrent impulses mirror the body's own bio-electrical field — so what we're doing isn't forcing anything artificial. We're restoring something that was already there.

What You Can Expect Across a Course

  • Sessions 1–3: You'll notice an immediate post-treatment lift and a beautiful glow. The results are temporary at this stage but each session feels progressively better.
  • Sessions 4–6: This is where things start to shift. The muscles are beginning to hold their position. Clients often notice their results lasting several days rather than fading overnight.
  • Sessions 7–10: The cumulative effect is fully established. Jawline definition, lifted brows, reduced fine lines and noticeably improved skin quality — results that hold and continue to improve with monthly maintenance.

Katy and I Have Seen This for Decades

My colleague Katy has been performing CACI since 1999 — between us, we have over 45 years of hands-on CACI experience. We were both trained and qualified directly by CACI themselves. We've seen this treatment evolve, and we've seen what it does for clients when they commit to a course properly.

That's why when a new client comes in for a consultation, we always take the time to explain this. A one-off is a lovely treat and a great introduction. But if you want the lift that makes your friends ask what you've had done — a course is where that happens.
