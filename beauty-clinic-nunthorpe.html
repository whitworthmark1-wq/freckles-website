Why Nunthorpe Clients Choose Freckles for CACI

CACI is a course, not a one-off — and the results depend on who's holding the wands. That's why clients from Nunthorpe, Marton and the villages towards Guisborough come into Middlesbrough for it: our CACI treatments are carried out by two practitioners trained directly by CACI, Katy since 1999 and Phillippa since 2002.

Every CACI journey at Freckles starts with a proper consultation. We'll look at your skin — usually with a Pro-A AI skin scan — talk through what you want to change, and recommend the right treatment and course, rather than selling you the most expensive option on the menu.

Most clients see a visible lift after their very first session, with results building over a course. You can read more about why on our Skin School: why a course of CACI beats a one-off facial.

Getting Here From Nunthorpe

Getting to Freckles From Nunthorpe

We're at 308 Linthorpe Road, Middlesbrough TS1 3QX — on the main road south of the town centre, close to Albert Park and the Dorman Museum.

By Car — Around 15 Minutes

Head north on the A172 through Marton towards Middlesbrough town centre, then across to Linthorpe Road. Outside rush hour it's a straightforward drive from Nunthorpe village or Swans Corner.

By Bus — Arriva 29

The 29 runs between Nunthorpe and Middlesbrough via Marton and Linthorpe, with stops on Linthorpe Road close to the clinic, including the Dorman Museum stop.

By Train or Parking

Nunthorpe station is on the Esk Valley line into Middlesbrough. If you're driving, there's on-street pay-and-display parking on Linthorpe Road and the side streets nearby.
