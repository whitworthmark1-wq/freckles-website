What Is Skin School?

Real Answers From the Experts Who Treat Your Skin Every Day

Skin School is where Phillippa and Katy share what they know — built up over a combined 20+ years of treating skin at Freckles Beauty Clinic on Linthorpe Road. Every article, guide and video answers a question our clients actually ask us. No marketing fluff. No generic advice. Just the real information you need to understand your skin and make the right treatment decisions.
