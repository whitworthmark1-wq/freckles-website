How CACI Works

Microcurrent Technology — The Science Behind the Lift at Freckles

As we age, natural electrical activity in our facial muscles diminishes. CACI delivers gentle, precise microcurrent impulses that mirror the body's own bio-electrical field, re-stimulating muscle activity and cellular regeneration.

Combined with LED light therapy, the impulses stimulate collagen production and accelerate tissue repair while lifting and retraining the underlying muscles — delivering both an immediate visible effect and cumulative long-term improvement.
